Overview

Summary

Today your team builds lists in Sales Navigator and Apollo, checks them against HubSpot and team links in separate places, never compares Apollo with Dripify, and spends hours rating leads that a bot trained on one founder persona gets mostly wrong. We are replacing that with one place to build every list: pick a persona, preview what will be searched, find the people, remove the ones you already know, rate everyone P1 to Negative, review only the borderline ones, and export. The foundation is already built, but so far it has been tested on invented data only, not on your accounts. These two weeks prove it on your own lists and hand it over.
