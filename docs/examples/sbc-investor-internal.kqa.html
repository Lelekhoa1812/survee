The persona, label, review, suppression and export foundation is built and tested with fakes. Overview

SBC’s rated lists (examples/ssf25)

32 rated lists, 34,180 people, 26,225 with a mark from SBC’s reviewers, across nine of the ten personas. These are the real examples the importer (B1) and the agreement report (B2) must read. Counts only here: the files hold personal data and stay out of git.

What the files look like

  • One sheet, “Fresh List”, from the earlier bot: linkedInProfileUrl, profileUrl, fullName, vmid, AI Rating, AI Reasoning, Feedback, then the Sales Navigator profile columns (company, industry, title, location, tenure, past roles). The oldest list has no AI Reasoning.
  • Bot labels in AI Rating: Positive1, Positive2, Edge Case, Negative, NA, plus error text on 240 rows (230 in the Exited Founder list). SBC’s labels in Feedback: P1, P2, Negative, NA and P-Female. SBC never writes Edge.
  • 34,098 of the 34,180 rows are located in Singapore (these are Singapore SPV lists). A separate SalesNav Archives folder holds the raw exports; the one stray Sales Navigator sheet has 33 rows and no rating.

What it means for the build

  • Importer (B1): map Positive1 to P1, Positive2 to P2, Edge Case to Edge, Negative and NA as they are, error text to “unrated”, and carry P-Female as its own mark. Never learn from it as a gender signal (B7). The old note that SBC has four marks was wrong: they use five, and none is Edge.
  • Agreement report (B2): report fit against not-fit first. Exact-label agreement is only measurable where SBC marked: all 6,393 marked bot Positives simply carry the bot’s own label, because nobody marks a Positive down, and another 1,766 were marked NA on one list. Treat that as an unchecked sample, not as accuracy (D16).
  • Training value: the 10,166 Negatives SBC moved to a fit are the corrections the rating needs. The bot’s own reasons show the cause: “employee in a non-leadership role” leads the list.
  • Failed ratings (B20): 240 rows came back as an API or JSON error and were left in the list. Retry, validate, and never export an unrated row as a rating.

Risks in the data

  • One list (1,900 rows) is marked NA throughout, including 1,556 the bot rated P2. Do not read it as a rating (D15).
  • Unmarked rows are 98% bot Positives, so SBC’s marks say almost nothing about Positives that are wrong (D16).
  • The 65% moved rate leans on two bulk-marked lists (one Partner, one Entrepreneur in Residence, 1,469 rows moved with no Negative left); without them it is about 61%. Per-list moved rates run from 13% to 100%, and reviewers differ widely, so read it as a pattern, not an error rate.
  • The lists are almost disjoint: 12 people appear twice, and 2 of them were marked a fit in one list and Negative in the other. P-Female is spread over 26 of 32 lists, but 322 of the 1,051 sit in one Partner list.
  • The keyword splits in the client file (non-profit, recruiters, one-person firms, advisers, job seekers) come from the bot’s reasoning text, so groups overlap and counts are indicative.
  • examples/ssf25/ and examples/investors/ are untracked, not ignored. They hold personal data: add them to .gitignore before anyone runs git add.
