A personal reflection

Make room for a next step

Use these prompts to think through something you would like to work on and shape a next step that suits you. You are in control: answer only what you want, in as much or as little detail as feels right.

Share only what you choose. This reflection is not clinical advice, a diagnosis, or an assessment. Avoid names and sensitive details. Your answers save as you go, and the person hosting this reflection can review and export them. Ask your host how they will use and retain responses. If you change an answer and a follow-up disappears, that answer is retained and may reappear if it becomes relevant again; use Review answers to inspect or clear retained answers.
