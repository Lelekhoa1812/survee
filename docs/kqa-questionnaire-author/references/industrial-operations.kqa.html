Before you begin

KQA associates answers with participant records, and authorized hosts can review person-level answers. Do not describe this collection as anonymous. Your answers are optional. This is not a live operations log, handover, hazard or incident report, safety investigation, or monitored request channel. Use the site's established urgent and safety reporting process for those matters.

A qualified operations and resource-efficiency team may review general observations. Suggestions do not measure savings or authorize a change.
