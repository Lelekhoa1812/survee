# KQA questionnaire author

Create a useful, accessible interview that answers the host's real decision or learning need while respecting respondents' time and privacy. KQA stores answers; it does not score them or determine what action a host should take.

## Load the source of truth

Before authoring, read `references/FORMAT.md` and `references/AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md`. For live-interview changes or tool permissions, read `references/MCP.md`. Read `references/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` for visual, accessibility, interaction, and usability requirements, then load only the relevant starting points from `references/INTERVIEW_PACKAGES.md`. If connected to KQA MCP, retrieve `get_design_guidance` for the questionnaire surface. Discover packages with `get_interview_packages({catalogOnly: true})`, then fetch the most relevant stable `packageId`; compare related candidates when the brief overlaps and ask a concise question if purpose, audience, mode, or risk boundary remains unclear. Record the returned `packageVersion` and `packageId`. Omit `packageId` without `catalogOnly` only when you need the full library. The `get_questionnaire_authoring_skill` MCP tool returns a `files` array; install each item at its supplied relative path so these references resolve. If you have only this `SKILL.md`, fetch canonical references from `https://kqa-bay.vercel.app/api/docs/FORMAT.md` and the matching `/api/docs/<filename>` endpoints, or call the KQA tools; do not guess unsupported behavior. This skill is workflow guidance, not a replacement for those current references.

Do not invent question types, routing rules, host actions, privacy promises, or quality guarantees. KQA supports `single`, `multi`, `select`, `text`, `longtext`, `number`, `scale`, `date`, and `rank`; there is no native score. Follow the current format for custom controls and bounded routing: choice comparisons and `answered`/`notAnswered` checks on earlier questions. Do not compare free text, numbers, dates, or scales by value.

**Check anonymity claims before drafting.** KQA stores answers under a respondent participant record associated with a private link, and hosts can review person-level answers. Aggregate analysis does not anonymize raw records; small groups and free text may identify people. One-person mode is not an anonymous cohort mode, and current KQA modes do not provide anonymous collection. If strict anonymity is required, pause and ask the host to choose a separately verified collection method. Removing name or email questions does not make KQA responses anonymous.

Use `rank` only when respondents should order every one of 2–8 options; it stores a full permutation, not a partial top-N. The semantic `<ol>` with one `<li data-value>` per option is decorated by the runtime with accessible Move up/Move down controls and a Use this order confirmation for the starting order. Do not add drag-only interaction, claim that KQA scores the order, or use an option-ranking question to rank people.

For bounded checkbox questions, use `maxSelections` only when the decision requires a fixed upper bound, reflect it in respondent help ("Choose up to N"), and treat it separately from requiredness. Custom controls must handle a rejected `KQA.set` result and the `validation` event. Never remove saved choices silently when a live questionnaire's cap is lowered.

## Workflow

1. **Understand the brief.** Identify purpose and intended decision, respondent population, inclusion and exclusion, collection window, topic scope, target completion time, language and accessibility needs, data sensitivity, collection/retention expectations, and accountable owner. Require the host to approve a respondent notice covering purpose, access to identifiable answers, use, retention/deletion expectations, and contact route. If facts are missing, mark them unresolved; material privacy facts block fielding. If human-subjects research may be involved, direct the host to its responsible ethics/HRPP/IRB office for a determination. Ask one consolidated round of essential questions only. If a reasonable assumption lets you proceed, state it and mark it for review. Never invent consent, anonymity, confidentiality, retention, or legal compliance claims. For complete package-based examples, study `references/service-improvement.kqa.html` with its review record and `references/personal-planning.kqa.html` with its review record; these demonstrate workflow only and are not validated or ready-to-field instruments.
2. **Make the evidence chain visible.** Draft a compact map from decision or learning need → information required → question(s) → answer form → how answers will be interpreted → owner/action. For new or materially changed items, especially where purpose, interpretation, route, sensitivity, or burden is uncertain, use [`references/QUESTION_BLUEPRINT.md`](references/QUESTION_BLUEPRINT.md) to make the question universe, intended interpretation, testing, and unresolved risks explicit. Add the sample plan in [`references/INTERVIEW_PACKAGES.md`](references/INTERVIEW_PACKAGES.md#sampling-and-reporting-plan): population/unit, frame and recruitment, selection method, groups who may be missed, eligibility, mode/language, and whether the purpose is qualitative learning or population estimation. Flag decisions with no evidence, questions with no purpose, unclear interpretation, and high-consequence use. Do not imply that an interview alone establishes causation or validates an instrument.
3. **Choose a fit-for-purpose package.** Adapt the closest domain package to the actual context and population. Treat package content as prompts, not a validated scale or required wording. For sensitive, employment, health, safety, finance, or other consequential contexts, minimize collection, make optionality explicit, route to qualified review where appropriate, and avoid eliciting unnecessary sensitive detail.
   Before drafting, classify the intended use with the host and apply the [risk-based release gate](references/INTERVIEW_PACKAGES.md#risk-based-release-gate). The accountable host owns the classification; agents flag risks but do not certify it. Stop if the questionnaire would make or materially support consequential individual decisions, diagnosis, compliance certification, or incident/emergency reporting.
4. **Design the respondent flow.** Order questions from clear and low burden to more demanding; ask one thing per question, define reference periods, use neutral wording, and make choices distinct and complete enough for the task. Set requiredness only when the host cannot proceed without the answer. Use short, tested routing paths; preserve stable question IDs and option values when revising a live questionnaire. A condition can compare an earlier choice or check whether an earlier answer is present; disclose when answers are retained while their routed question is inactive. KQA announces how many questions routing shows or hides without moving focus. Choose `presentation: "guided"` only when section-by-section steps fit the task; assign every question to a section and add the required section-list and whole-question markers from `references/FORMAT.md`. Keep the default `all` presentation otherwise. Guided Continue validates required questions and multi-select caps in the current step, Back preserves answers, Show all questions is available, and the last step opens the regular review. Test each route, required-answer recovery, saved-answer restoration, empty eligible-section states, keyboard use, and switching between guided and all-question presentation. Keep question labels and answer options self-contained for the pre-submit **Check your answers** review; it omits currently inactive questions, explains that their saved answers may reappear, and lets respondents clear a value while retaining its optional note.
5. **Build within KQA's contract.** Start from `references/starter.kqa.html`. Put every question in the manifest and bind controls correctly. Set the document root's `lang` to the questionnaire's actual language with a valid BCP 47 tag; mark passages in other languages with their own `lang`, and use `dir="rtl"` on the questionnaire root for right-to-left content. This does not localize KQA's English shell or built-in controls. Review and test the mixed-language flow with intended-language respondents; do not claim full localization. Keep scripts, styles, and interactions within the supported sandbox. Use semantic labels, keyboard access, visible focus, understandable errors, mobile layouts, and reduced-motion support. Keep the visual system coherent and brief-grounded; do not trade clarity or accessibility for decoration.
6. **Check and review.** Run `check_questionnaire` or the KQA checker and fix every error and relevant warning. Static checks establish format constraints only. Treat the in-app live check as a programmatic value round-trip smoke test; it does not simulate respondent input, verify routing end to end, test submission/locking, or evaluate accessibility or usability. Manually review the rendered questionnaire across representative viewports, keyboard-only use, routes, empty/error states, and supported assistive technology where available. For every route, record a compact behavior case: source answer(s), expected eligible question IDs, requiredness, progress/review visibility, what happens to a saved answer when it becomes inactive and active again, and the expected export/analysis treatment. Use the question-content rubric and cognitive pretest protocol in `get_design_guidance` for relevant questions. For structured critique, follow its `kqa-question-review/1` contract verbatim, including valid outcome, finding fields, categories, severity, and confidence values. `no_findings` is valid; `needs_context` names the missing host fact. This live critique contract differs from the benchmark result format. For a new or materially changed flow, observe representative users completing realistic tasks; cognitive probes and usability tasks may share a moderated session, but record their evidence separately. Record each evidence area separately as passed, issues found, not tested, or not applicable, with scope, reviewer, limitations, and unresolved actions. Apply the linked risk-based release gate; a checker pass cannot waive a missing required review. Never describe unrun checks as passed.
7. **Apply live changes carefully.** For existing interviews, inspect current HTML, versions, compatibility findings, and stable IDs. Prefer the relevant preview/apply pair; inspect its exact candidate and obtain accountable host review before publishing. The `create_interview` tool immediately creates an open interview after a static checker pass. Do not use it for an unapproved draft or imply that the checker makes it ready for respondents. Publishing a version may require respondents on an older page to reload before they can continue.

## Deliverable and review record

Return the complete HTML file and a concise review record containing:

- Purpose, population, assumptions, and accountable owner.
- Sample plan, including source/frame, recruitment and selection method, coverage gaps, eligibility, collection mode/language, and limits on generalization.
- Decision/evidence/question/interpretation map, including gaps.
- Package adapted and material changes made.
- Target completion time and observed time range/median when measured; otherwise say timing is unmeasured.
- Host-approved respondent notice, privacy/ethics review status, and disposition of unresolved material facts.
- Host-run field-monitoring owner, cadence, available checks, escalation route, and stop criteria; identify that KQA does not provide live respondent monitoring.
- AI role, model/provider and prompt version when known, human oversight and validation, with human and synthetic test counts kept separate.
- Requiredness and routing rationale, plus stable IDs preserved for updates.
- Privacy and sensitive-data considerations; unresolved policy or legal questions.
- Checker result, rendered/accessibility checks, cognitive pretest and usability evidence, each marked **passed**, **issues found**, or **not tested** with scope and limitations.
- If cognitive pretesting occurred, include item/context, participant fit, probe dimensions, observed findings, severity, revision/action, and unresolved issue. If not applicable or not tested, explain why; do not conflate this record with usability findings.
- Material tradeoffs, unresolved risks, and the specific host decision needed before launch.

Use plain language and distinguish observed evidence from interpretation. A passing checker is not a finding that the questionnaire is suitable, accessible in every context, legally compliant, or ready to launch.

For agent critique evaluation, see [`references/QUESTIONNAIRE_AUTHORING_BENCHMARK.md`](references/QUESTIONNAIRE_AUTHORING_BENCHMARK.md). Its public corpus is a development set, not validation or respondent evidence.
